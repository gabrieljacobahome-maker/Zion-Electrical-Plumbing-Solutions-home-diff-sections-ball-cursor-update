Who We Are
About Zion Electrical & Plumbing Solutions

Zion Electrical & Plumbing Solutions is a premier, integrated utility contractor based in Coimbatore, bringing together decades of hands-on technical expertise, skilled craftsmanship, and an unflinching commitment to quality. Headquartered in Ganapathi Maanagar near Prozone Mall, Zion was established on a vision of delivering seamless, high-precision electrical and plumbing solutions for both residential and commercial properties across the region.

Led by founder Joseph Jasudhas, who brings over 30 years of rich industry experience, Zion operates with a robust workforce of more than 20 dedicated staff members and technicians. Their extensive service capabilities cover complete domestic wiring, circuit diagnostics, power engineering, pipe network installations, deep leak detection, and full-scale commercial projects throughout Ganapathy and greater Coimbatore.

The company's focus on operational excellence has earned it an outstanding reputation, reflected in consistent 4.7★ ratings across major review platforms. Clients consistently highlight the team's rapid response times, deep investigative troubleshooting, and highly professional, humble conduct on every single job site.

Operating daily until 9:00 PM, Zion continues to set the benchmark for local service contractors. Their business is built on a simple, timeless principle: "Every client's trust is earned through quality workmanship, prompt service, and lasting relationships."

For reliable, top-tier utility engineering and quick project turnarounds, Zion Electrical & Plumbing Solutions remains Coimbatore's most dependable partner.
